Hermes privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Alastair Gray. No one else can sign in to it.

What Hermes accesses

With the owner's permission, Hermes reads and writes events in the owner's own primary Google Calendar (scope calendar.events). It uses this only to find free times for meetings the owner is asked to attend, and to add, move or remove events the owner has chosen.

Where data goes

Calendar data is processed on the owner's home server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Event details are not sent to any AI model.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Hermes keeps only the bookings it made and the scheduling preferences the owner confirmed. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.
